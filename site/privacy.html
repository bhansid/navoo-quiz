2. How we use your information

  • Your report. Your report is generated automatically from your answers using fixed rules written by our team. No artificial intelligence or human reviews your answers to create it. A copy is sent to the email address you give us.
  • Launch updates. We email you about Navoo’s launch, early access and helpful content. Every email lets you unsubscribe.
  • Measuring and improving. We look at sign-ups, answers and traffic sources in aggregate to improve the website and our future product.
  • Advertising. We tell advertising platforms when someone signs up so they can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested.
  • Security. We use basic signals (for example, rate limits and spam checks) to protect the site from abuse.

3. Legal basis (UK and EU visitors)

We process your information because you asked us to (create and send your report and add you to the waitlist), on the basis of your consent for marketing emails and non-essential cookies, and for our legitimate interests in measuring and improving our marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

4. Cookies and tracking

We use the following technologies, where enabled:

  • Meta Pixel: records page views and sign-ups so Meta can measure our ads. It sets cookies such as _fbp.
  • Meta Conversions API: when you sign up we send Meta your email address in hashed (scrambled, irreversible) form, together with your IP address, browser user agent and Meta cookie IDs, so Meta can match the sign-up to an ad click.
  • Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads: measure traffic and conversions, and may set cookies such as _ga.
  • Your browser’s session storage: remembers your quiz answers and where you came from while the tab is open. It’s cleared when you close the tab.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, opt out of personalised ads in your Facebook ad preferences and Google ad settings, and use the Google Analytics opt-out.

We keep your sign-up, answers and report details until Navoo launches and for up to 24 months afterwards, or until you ask us to delete them or unsubscribe, whichever is sooner. Analytics and advertising data is kept by Meta and Google according to their own retention settings.

9. Children

Navoo is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

10. Security

11. Health information

The quiz asks about everyday habits, not medical conditions. Your report contains general wellbeing guidance and is not medical advice. If you have a health condition, please speak to a healthcare professional before making changes.
